About Me

I’m Wellesley Kerr, a dedicated music educator and performer based in Vancouver, BC. With a dual degree in Music and Education from the University of British Columbia and ARCT certifications in both piano and flute performance, I bring a deep passion for teaching and a strong foundation in music theory, history, and instrumental instruction.

I’ve taught over 40 students through my private studio and led ensembles across school settings from elementary to secondary levels including York House School and Point Grey Secondary School. Whether in the classroom or on stage, my mission is to inspire students through meaningful, personalized music education that nurtures both technical skill and artistic expression.
